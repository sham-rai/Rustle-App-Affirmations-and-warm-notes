**What this says:**
1. Demand for emotional support from software is **big and proven**. People already use general chatbots for it, and generic affirmation apps make millions.
2. **Incumbents are adding memory** (Headspace Ebb, Day One, ChatGPT). "AI + memory" alone won't be a differentiator for long. **Experience, focus and trust will be.**
3. The App Store is **extremely crowded**. Distribution strategy matters as much as the product.

**Rustle's white space: push-based and deeply personal.** Nobody owns "it comes to you, and it knows you." Research found **no major app** that combines rich onboarding + check-ins + a notes board, **persistent memory** across all of them, and **delivery of freshly generated personal notes via notifications and widgets.**

### 2.3 Lessons from competitors

1. **I am / Motivation:** the notification + widget + beautiful card + category habit loop is proven at 10M+ installs. **Copy the delivery mechanics and replace the content with real personalisation.**
2. **Finch:** emotional attachment drives retention and word-of-mouth among Gen Z. It's also a warning: it's playful, and our warmth must be *genuine*, not gamified.
3. **Rosebud:** investors fund "AI that remembers you." Rosebud wins with people who journal. **Rustle wins with people who don't want to do work.**
4. **Woebot:** a clinical AI chat in B2C shut down. **Stay non-clinical.**
5. **Replika / Character.AI:** chat companions attract lawsuits and regulation. **Stay non-chat and non-romantic.**
6. **Day One / Headspace:** incumbents are adding memory. **Move fast, and build brand and community.**

## 5. Market entry recommendation

- **Launch languages: English + French.** English markets (US, UK, IE, CA, AU) give the biggest revenue pool. French markets (France, Quebec, Belgium, Switzerland) are **less crowded**: 48% of French adults already use a digital mental-health tool (IFOP/MentalTech, Dec 2025), but local players are meditation (Petit BamBou) or CBT/therapy-style (MindDay, Mon Sherpa), and French affirmation apps are mostly translations. See [14-audience-and-segments.md](14-audience-and-segments.md) and [16-competitor-comparison.md](16-competitor-comparison.md).
- **Soft launch in Canada** (English + French in one market), then the US/UK plus France, Belgium and Switzerland.
- **Wedge niches:** exam season (Oct–Dec, Apr–Jun) and breakups/divorce (with peaks in January and after Valentine's Day, and the summer's end).
- **Time the launch** to a natural peak: early **January** (the "new year, hard time" plus post-holiday breakups season, and the January exams in France) or **late April** (the finals season).
