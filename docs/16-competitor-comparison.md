**Apple's App Store age ratings changed in 2025:** the new tiers are **4+, 9+, 13+, 16+, 18+** (12+ and 17+ were removed), and all developers had to answer the new questionnaire, including new questions about **medical or wellness content**, by 31 Jan 2026 ([Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=ks775ehf), [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/07/25/apple-overhauls-app-store-age-ratings/)). **Rustle should choose 18+.**

### The trend
The industry is **moving toward restricting minors** in emotional AI (Character.AI's ban, ChatGPT's teen mode, Ebb 18+, and fines for weak age checks), driven by regulators (FTC inquiry, California SB 243, the Italian fine) and lawsuits.

### What 18+ means for Rustle

| ✅ Advantages | ⚠️ Trade-offs |
|---|---|
| Avoids most minor-specific obligations (COPPA; SB 243 minor provisions; GDPR parental consent, which is under 15 in France and under 14 in Quebec) | We lose teenagers (a big audience for Finch and "I am"), including many high-school exam students (e.g. the French bac) |
| Aligns with the most responsible players (Ebb) and with where regulation is going | An age gate adds a little friction |
| Safer content and tone decisions (adult topics like divorce and illness) | Self-declared age can be faked, so we need reasonable measures |
| A stronger trust story for press, partners and B2B | — |

### How to implement 18+ reasonably (learning from the Replika fine)
1. **App Store rating 18+** and **Google Play rating** set appropriately (Mature 17+ or the equivalent), with a target-audience declaration of 18+.
2. **A neutral date-of-birth gate** before onboarding (not a leading "Are you 18? Yes/No"). Block under-18s with a kind screen and youth resources, and don't let them retry immediately (store a flag locally).
3. **Ongoing signals:** the safety classifier flags "sounds under 18" (mentions of school year, parents' rules and so on) → a gentle re-confirmation; for repeated signals, limit the account and show youth resources.
4. **Apple Declared Age Range API / Google Play age signals** where available, as extra, privacy-friendly signals (verify availability per region at build time).
5. **Document it** in the DPIA: why self-declaration plus signals is proportionate for a non-chat, non-romantic, adult wellness app.

## 4. Where Rustle wins, and where it must be careful

**Wins**
- **The only app that is personal *and* comes to you**, with no journaling or chatting required.
- **Knows your dates and people**, so notes are timely (no competitor does this well).
- **French market:** no strong personal-AI-support player. Petit BamBou is meditation, MindDay and Mon Sherpa are CBT/therapy-style, and the AI "psy" chats are chat tools. Rustle's non-clinical, non-chat, privacy-first positioning is open territory.
- **Safety by design** (no chat, 18+, no romance), which fits the regulatory direction.

**Be careful**
- **Headspace and Day One have brand trust and are adding memory.** Rustle must feel warmer and more personal from minute one.
- **Finch owns "cute + caring" among young users.** Don't compete on gamification.
- **ChatGPT is free and already used for emotional support by ~20% of under-30s.** Rustle must show what ChatGPT doesn't do: it remembers *without being asked*, it writes first, it's timed to your life, and it's beautiful.
- **In France, don't look like a "psy" app.** MindDay and Mon Sherpa already frame themselves around therapy techniques, and French law and culture are sensitive to health claims (see doc 11).
