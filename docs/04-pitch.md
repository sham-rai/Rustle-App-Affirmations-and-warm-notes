# 04 · Pitch

## Elevator pitch (30 seconds)

> When you're going through something hard (losing a job, a breakup or divorce, an illness, caring for someone, exams), the last thing you need is generic advice or a chatbot you have to keep talking to.
> **Rustle** is a mobile app that listens once and remembers. You tell it what's happening in two minutes, drop thoughts on a private notes board whenever you want, and Rustle leaves you short, warm, *specific* notes through the day. It knows Thursday is your interview, it remembers your sister's surgery, and a month later it shows you how far you've come.
> Affirmation apps make millions with generic quotes. We make them personal. **Less generation, more relationship.**

## Pitch deck outline (12 slides)

**1. Title:** Rustle, *notes that know what you're going through.* A lock-screen image: "Thursday's the interview. You already did the brave part."

**2. Problem:** Hard times are lonely.
- 41–67% of Gen Z report loneliness (depending on the survey).
- Friends get tired, therapy is expensive and slow, and chatbots demand conversation.
- Affirmation apps are generic: "I am worthy" doesn't know your big interview is tomorrow.

**3. Insight:** Being *remembered* is what makes support feel real. People don't need more words. They need the *right* words about *their* life, at the right moment, with zero effort.

**4. Solution:** Rustle.
- **Listen:** 2-min onboarding, 30-second check-ins, a private notes board.
- **Remember:** a memory engine for people, dates and situations.
- **Return:** personal notes via notifications and widgets, plus a note back when you write.
- **Reflect:** a monthly "Look how far you've come."

**5. Demo:** onboarding → first note referencing the user's first job interview since a layoff → the Thursday 8:00 notification on interview day → board note + reply → recap.

**6. Why now**
- LLMs write warm, nuanced text for under $1 per user per month.
- Incumbents are proving demand for AI + memory (Headspace Ebb memory in Dec 2025, Day One AI in Apr 2026, Rosebud's $6M seed) but all of them are *pull*-based and *effort*-based.
- Regulation is pushing chat companions toward caution. A non-chat, non-clinical design is structurally safer.

**7. Market**
- Mental wellness apps: ~$9–10B (2026), growing 14–17% a year.
- Affirmation apps prove willingness to pay: "I am" has ~10M installs and an estimated multi-million annual revenue.
- AI companionship is exploding (e.g. 41% of Character.AI users use it for emotional support).
- Wedge: students and the heartbroken → expand to divorce, layoffs, illness and caregiving → B2B2C.

**8. Competition:** a 2×2 of Personal vs Generic × Push vs Pull. Rustle alone owns *personal + push*.

**9. Business model**
- Freemium plus a 7-day trial on annual. $7.99/mo or $44.99/yr.
- AI cost ≈ $0.5–1.0 per paying user per month → 65–85% contribution margin.
- Later: gifting, B2B2C (universities, outplacement, employers, patient organisations).

**10. Go-to-market**
- Built-in virality: *Send a warm note* (friend-to-friend), share cards, monthly recap shares.
- TikTok/Reels content engine (study-tok, heartbreak-tok) plus micro-creators.
- ASO for "affirmations" (a high-volume keyword), Apple editorial moments.
- Launch in English + French (US/UK/CA/AU + France/Quebec/Belgium/Switzerland); seasonal peaks (exams, January, Valentine's).

**11. Traction / plan** *(fill in after the concierge test and beta)*
- Concierge test: X people, Y% felt "seen", Z% wanted to pay.
- Waitlist: N signups.
- Roadmap: MVP in ~18 weeks → recap → Android widget → voice → B2B2C.

**12. Team & ask**
- Founder(s), advisors (clinical psychologist, AI/mobile engineer).
- *Ask (if raising):* €250–500k pre-seed for 18 months: 2 engineers, a designer, a growth lead and a content budget. Milestones: 250k installs, $30k MRR, B2B pilots.
