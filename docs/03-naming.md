# 03 · Naming Analysis

Candidates: **Rustle · Nautila · Zorya · Celeste · Oberih**

> Conflict checks used web and App Store searches only (September 2026). **Before committing, run a formal trademark search** (USPTO, EUIPO/TMview, WIPO Global Brand Database, **INPI** for France, **CIPO** for Canada) in classes 9 (apps), 42 (SaaS) and 44/45 (wellness/personal services), and check domains and social handles.

## 1. Criteria

| Criterion | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Meaning fit** | Does it carry the promise (warmth, being heard, notes, growth, protection)? |
| **Say-it / spell-it test** | Can someone hear it on TikTok and find it in the App Store? |
| **Global pronunciation** | Works in English and doesn't sound strange in key languages (**French first**, then ES/DE/PT…) |
| **Distinctiveness / conflicts** | Trademark risk, App Store confusion, SEO competition |
| **Brand elasticity** | Works as a verb or noun ("send a Rustle"), supports visual identity |
| **Domain / handles** | `.app` / `.com` variants, @handles |

## 3. Candidate-by-candidate

### Rustle ✅ Recommended
- **Meaning:** the soft sound of leaves and paper, of a note being unfolded. It's quiet, alive and intimate. It fits the "notes" metaphor perfectly ("a rustle of paper, someone left you a note") and a nature-and-growth visual world.
- **Pros:** a short real English word, easy to say and spell, onomatopoeic (you can *hear* the brand, which suits a sound-branded notification). It works as a noun: *"I got a Rustle this morning"*, *"send someone a Rustle."* The house emoji 🌿 and a leaf icon come naturally.
- **Cons:** "rustle" also means stealing livestock (*cattle rustling*) and "rustle up" (to make something quickly). These are mild and rarely a problem. Some non-native speakers may not know the word, but it's easy to learn.
- **Conflicts found:** "Rustle Up – Recipes" (App Store), "Rustle: Idle Crafting" (game), an Instagram handle @rustleapp (unclear use). **No wellness app found.** Low–moderate risk.
- **Tip:** use a descriptor in the App Store title for search: **"Rustle: Personal Affirmations"** or **"Rustle – Notes That Know You."** Domain ideas: `rustle.app`, `getrustle.com`, `rustlenotes.com`.

### Nautila 🥈 Strong runner-up (the best *story*)
- **Meaning:** from the *nautilus*, a shell that grows in chambers, building a new, bigger chamber as it grows **while keeping every old one**. It's a beautiful metaphor for the "look how far you've come" and "nothing is erased, we grow" philosophy. The golden spiral is instantly visual.
- **Pros:** a unique invented spelling (no exact app match found), elegant, feminine-leaning but soft, and a strong logo (the spiral).
- **Cons:** people will spell it "Nautilus"/"Nautilla" and pronunciation varies (NAW-ti-la vs nau-TEE-la). It sounds more oceanic or tech than warm. **Nautilus** is a well-known fitness brand (trademark assets sold in 2024), so there's some residual association in health/fitness.
- **Idea:** even if you don't use it as the app name, **use the nautilus as the visual metaphor for the monthly recap** ("your spiral this month").

### Zorya ⚠️ Not recommended
- **Meaning:** the Slavic goddess/personification of the dawn (Зоря, "dawn"/"star"). Hope after the night is a beautiful meaning.
- **Cons:** hard for English speakers (ZOR-ya? zor-YA?) and hard to spell after hearing it. **Several existing apps use this name**, including a "Zorya" personal-companion journal/AI chat app, which is *close to our concept*. Also a social app, an HR platform and a Steam game. **Moderate-to-high conflict risk.**
- **Possible use:** name a **theme** ("Zorya — dawn theme") or the morning note ("your Zorya note").

### Celeste ❌ Not recommended
- **Meaning:** "heavenly", a soft night-sky feel; a known name in many languages.
- **Cons, significant ones:**
  1. ***Celeste*, the acclaimed 2018 video game,** is literally about anxiety, depression and climbing a mountain. It dominates search and has a devoted community, so there's brand confusion and SEO trouble.
  2. **"Celeste – Life Guide"**, an **AI emotional-support / life-coach app, is already on the App Store**, a direct functional overlap.
  3. An astrology app named Celeste and a "Céleste Wellness" app also exist.
- **Verdict:** high conflict risk and no distinctiveness. Use it as a **theme name** at most ("Celeste — night sky theme").

### Oberih (Оберіг) ❌ Not as the global name / ✅ as a Ukrainian touch
- **Meaning:** a Ukrainian protective amulet or talisman. The emotional meaning ("something that protects you, made with love") is the **most fitting of all** for Ukrainians.
- **Cons:** very hard to pronounce and spell for non-Ukrainians (Oberih / Oberig / Obereg). The **"Oberih" state conscription/military registry system** in Ukraine now dominates the word in Ukrainian search and news, which is a confusing and even stressful association for the very audience that would understand the meaning. Jewelry brands use "Oberig".
- **Possible use:** with an English + French launch, Oberih has no meaning for the target audience. At most, it could be the name of a "protective notes" collection for hard days if a Ukrainian localisation is added later.

## 5. Recommendation

1. **Go with Rustle** as the product name, pending trademark clearance. Use the App Store subtitle for keywords: *"Rustle — Affirmations that know you"* or *"Personal notes & affirmations."*
2. **Use the other names inside the brand world:** themes "Zorya" (dawn) and "Celeste" (night sky); the "Nautilus spiral" recap visual; (Oberih only if a Ukrainian localisation comes later).
3. **Secure** `rustle.app` (or `getrustle.app`), `@rustleapp` / `@rustle.notes` handles on TikTok, Instagram and X, and file an EU + US trademark (class 9/42) once you're confident. Budget about €1–3k.
4. **If trademark checks fail for Rustle** → fall back to **Nautila**.
